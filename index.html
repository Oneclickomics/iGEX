A CLOSER LOOK

THE iGEX WORKSPACES

Your data.
One connected workspace.

Manage your datasets in the iGEX Explorer and choose a workflow to visualize your data.

Start with the data you want to understand.

Build your iGEX library by importing public datasets or your own experimental data. Perform quality checks, create reusable gene lists, and organize genes and experiments into custom groups. Select the data you want to explore and send it directly to one of the analysis studios.

Make complex data easier to understand.

Explore variation across genes and experiments using principal component analysis. Identify clusters, investigate outliers, and add additional sampling data for more context. Customize your plots to highlight patterns and create clear, publication-ready figures.

Bring your data into focus.

Visualize transcriptomic and proteomic abundance data using interactive heatmaps. Perform correlation analysis to identify gene relationships and apply clustering methods to compare expression patterns. Use Orthoscope to find gene orthologs and explore similar relationships across species. Customize your heatmaps to create the perfect plot for your next publication.

Identify experimental conditions that affect your genes.

Explore changes in gene expression across experimental conditions and genotypes. Screen thousands of differential expression datasets to identify conditions where your genes show the greatest changes. Use Orthoscope to investigate how orthologous genes respond in other species, and customize your volcano plots to highlight your findings.
